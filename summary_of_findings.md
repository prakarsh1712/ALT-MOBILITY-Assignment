
# Summary of Findings – Alt Mobility Data Analysis

## 1. Order & Sales Trends
- Order distribution is balanced: Pending (5K), Shipped (4.8K), Delivered (5K).
- Revenue has grown steadily, peaking over ₹64,000 in Mar 2025.

## 2. Customer Analysis
- 4,402 customers made multiple purchases.
- Monthly customer activity is stable.
- Opportunity: Retention programs to improve customer lifetime value.

## 3. Payment Insights
- Only 33% of payments are successful.
- High rate of `failed` and `pending` payments—critical issue to resolve.

## 4. Detailed Reporting
- Combined data reveals trends across order status and payment completion.
- Enables granular performance tracking by business segment.

## 5. Customer Retention
- Cohort retention drops below 5% after 2nd month.
- Month 1: 100%, Month 2: ~2.5% average return rate.
- Long-term loyalty programs could improve retention.

## Recommendations
- Fix payment gateway failure rates.
- Introduce post-purchase engagement (email follow-ups, incentives).
- Segment loyal customers and provide tailored offers.
